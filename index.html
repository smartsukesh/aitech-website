Prompt & AI Art Studio

We are building a tool to help AI enthusiasts and software developers create effective prompts and AI-generated artwork.

What it does

The project aims to make it easier to work with large language models (LLMs) and APIs when developing prompts and generating artwork with AI models. Our goal is to help users experiment with ideas and improve their creative workflow.

Current status

This is an early-stage project under development. We are working on its features and exploring how best to support prompt creation and AI-generated images.

About us

Built for people who like to experiment.

AI ART STUDIO is an early-stage project for AI enthusiasts and software developers. Its goal is to make experimenting with prompts, language models, APIs, and AI-generated imagery feel more accessible.

We're still developing the project and deciding which ideas to build first. This website introduces the direction we're exploring and will evolve as the project grows.

Where things stand: This is an informational website, not yet an interactive prompt or image-generation tool.
